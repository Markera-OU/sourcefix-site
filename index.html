FAQ

Does SourceFix make my site compliant?

No. SourceFix fixes common accessibility problems and publishes a dated statement of what was fixed. It does not certify compliance, and no tool can.

Does it change my posts without asking?

No. Fixes sit in an approval queue. You review and approve each one. Paid plans can auto-apply only low-risk fixes.

Which WordPress versions does it need?

WordPress 6.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later.

What does the statement say?

It lists what was fixed and when, on a public page on your site.
