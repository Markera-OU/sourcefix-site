How it works

Accessibility fixes happen where content is made, not in a separate audit months later.

1

Publish as usual

When you publish or update a post or page, SourceFix scans it for missing alt text, generic link text like “click here”, and heading problems such as skipped levels or missing headings.

2

Review AI-drafted fixes

The AI drafts a fix for each issue using the surrounding content, and queues it for your review. Edit the wording if you like. Nothing changes on your site until you approve it.

3

Approve and publish proof

Approve in one click. Low-risk fixes can apply automatically on paid plans. SourceFix re-publishes a dated public accessibility statement and keeps an audit log of every fix.

What SourceFix fixes

The common issues that make up the bulk of detectable accessibility failures on real sites.

Alt text for images

AI-written alt text based on the image and the surrounding copy. Decorative images are left with an empty alt, as they should be.

Descriptive link text

Replaces “click here”, “read more”, and bare URLs with link text that says where the link goes.

Heading structure

Fixes missing headings, multiple top-level headings, and skipped heading levels so the page outline reads correctly.

Approval queue

Every AI fix waits for your approval. You stay in control of what changes on your site, and the queue is one click per fix.

Dated accessibility statement

A public statement page, re-dated whenever fixes are applied, plus an audit log showing what was fixed and when.

Per-site memory

On Studio and Agency plans, SourceFix learns your site’s terminology and voice, so drafts sound like you wrote them.

Frequently asked questions

Does SourceFix make my site compliant?

No. SourceFix finds and fixes common accessibility issues, but compliance depends on your whole site, your theme, and your content. We never claim a site is compliant, and the public statement says so plainly.

Does the AI change my content without asking?

No. Every fix waits in the approval queue. On the free plan, all fixes wait. On paid plans, only low-risk fixes can apply automatically, and you can turn that off.

What does SourceFix send to the AI?

Only the text around an issue, so the AI can draft a fitting fix. No user data, no visitor data, and no whole-site exports.

Which WordPress versions are supported?

WordPress 6.0 and later, PHP 7.4 and later.

What is the accessibility statement?

A public page on your site that lists what SourceFix has fixed and when it was last updated. It is a record of work done, not a claim of compliance.
