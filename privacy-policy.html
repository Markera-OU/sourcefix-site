What SourceFix
does with data.

This page describes, plainly, what SourceFix sends, stores, and keeps on your own site. It is not a statement of compliance with any law or standard.

Effective 11 November 2026. SourceFix is a product of Markera OÜ, Sepapaja tn 6, Tallinn 15551, Estonia. Markera OÜ is the data controller for the service described below. Questions about this policy: support@usesourcefix.com.

What the plugin sends

When SourceFix authors a fix, it sends the text around an image and the list of headings on the page to its AI authoring service, hosted at backend-production-2abf.up.railway.app. That service forwards them to DeepSeek, the AI provider, so the service can write alt text and correct heading structure.

On Studio and Agency plans, your site’s terminology list is sent with the request, so the AI writes in your site’s own words. No other content is sent.

What the service stores

Only three things: your site’s address, its plan, and how many AI fixes it has used this month (to enforce the Free plan’s 50-fix monthly cap). It stores no post content and no fix history.

What stays on your site

Your approval history (which fixes were approved or rejected), your audit log, and your terminology list are stored only on your own site, in its WordPress database. They are never sent to the authoring service.

Payments and licensing

Paid plans are sold and licensed through Freemius. SourceFix does not process or store your payment details; payment processing is handled by Freemius, and Freemius’s own privacy policy governs what it collects when you buy or manage a license.

This website

usesourcefix.com sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads; Google may receive your IP address and browser details as part of that request.

Retention

Usage counts are kept per month, for as long as needed to enforce the monthly cap. No post content is stored at all, so there is nothing to delete on that side. Approval history, audit logs, and terminology live in your own WordPress database and are yours to delete from your site at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data the authoring service holds about your site, correct it, or delete it. You can also ask for a copy of it, or object to how it is used. Write to support@usesourcefix.com and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it, and the current version will always be the one on this page.
